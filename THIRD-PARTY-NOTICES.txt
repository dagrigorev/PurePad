PurePad — Third-Party Notices
=============================

Material Design Icons (Pictogrammers)
-------------------------------------
The folder-tree file/folder icons are rendered from icon path data taken from
Material Design Icons by Pictogrammers (https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/).

Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign/blob/master/LICENSE

The embedded path data lives in src/PurePad/View/Controls/MaterialIcons.cs and
is rasterized/tinted at runtime by FileIconRenderer.
